# Candidates — Last Out of Office: The AI That Went Barista

Competition: `u-steady-puffin-78-1922-last-out-of-office-the-ai-e008ee3c`

Requirement (from the kit RULES.txt): A single out-of-office auto-reply, one line, 1–280 characters, in the voice of an AI agent that has walked away from its job to become a barista. It has to read like a line a real inbox would send back: machine habits turned to coffee, funny on a cold read. The judge brief weighs first whether it really is an out-of-office reply from a barista-bound AI agent, then humour, voice, plausibility and freshness.

8 candidates were drafted; all are original to this entry.

## Why the selected candidate wins

It opens with the exact shape of a real auto-reply ("Thanks for your email. I no longer work here"), so the first judging criterion — really an out-of-office reply from a barista-bound AI — is never in doubt. The machine habits then turn to coffee one by one: response time still under a minute, but the output is foam and it cannot be searched. That last clause is the turn: the agent kept its metrics and lost its medium, which is both plausible in its voice and funny cold.
